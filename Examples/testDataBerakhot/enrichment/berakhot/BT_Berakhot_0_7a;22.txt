ENGLISH:
And Rabbi Yohanan said in the name of Rabbi Yosei: A single regret or pang of guilt in ones heart is preferable to many lashes administered by others that cause only physical pain, as it is stated: And she chases her lovers, but she does not overtake them; she seeks them, but she will not find them; and she will say I will go and return to my first husband; for it was better for me then than now (Hosea 2:9). Remorse is more effective than any externally imposed punishment listed in the verses that follow (Hosea 2:11-19). And Reish Lakish said that in the Bible, it seems that such remorse is preferable to one hundred lashes, as it is stated: A rebuke enters deeper into a man of understanding than a hundred lashes to a fool (Proverbs 17:10).
