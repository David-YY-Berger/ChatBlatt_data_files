ENGLISH:
And Rabbi Levi bar Hama said that Rabbi Shimon ben Lakish said: God said to Moses, Ascend to me on the mountain and be there, and I will give you the stone tablets and the Torah and the mitzva that I have written that you may teach them (Exodus 24:12), meaning that God revealed to Moses not only the Written Torah, but all of Torah, as it would be transmitted through the generations. The tablets are the ten commandments that were written on the tablets of the Covenant, the Torah is the five books of Moses. The mitzva is the Mishna, which includes explanations for the mitzvot and how they are to be performed. That I have written refers to the Prophets and Writings, written with divine inspiration. That you may teach them refers to the Talmud, which explains the Mishna. These explanations are the foundation for the rulings of practical halakha . This verse teaches that all aspects of Torah were given to Moses from Sinai.
