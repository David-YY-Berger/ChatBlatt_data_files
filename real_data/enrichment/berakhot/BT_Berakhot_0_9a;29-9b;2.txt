that righteous person, Abraham, will not say: God fulfilled His pronouncement: And they will be enslaved and afflicted, but God did not fulfill His pronouncement: And afterward, they will leave with great possessions. As God said to Abraham: Surely you shall know that your descendants will be foreigners in a land that is not theirs, and they will be enslaved and afflicted for four hundred years. And also that nation who enslaves them will I judge. And afterward, they will leave with great possessions (Genesis 15:13-14).The school of Rabbi Yannai continues: Israel said to Moses: If only we could get out ourselves. The Gemara offers a parable to one who was incarcerated in prison, and people would say to him: We promise, we will release you tomorrow and give you much money. He says to them: I beseech you, release me today and I ask for nothing. So too, Israel preferred leaving immediately empty handed rather than leaving later with great riches.
